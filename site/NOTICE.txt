MMPX (crates/u6-render/src/hd/mmpx.rs, tools/mmpx)
  The MMPX pixel-art magnifier: Morgan McGuire and Mara Gagiu, "MMPX Style-Preserving Pixel Art
  Magnification", Journal of Graphics Techniques, 2021,
  https://casual-effects.com/research/McGuire2021PixelArt/index.html
  Ported to Rust from the MMPXAlgorithm class of the reference C++ in the paper's supplement
  (code/source/cppPerf.cpp). tools/mmpx/gen-vectors.sh fetches that supplement to make the test
  vectors; the supplement itself is not in this repository.

Galmuri7 (site/fonts, tools/font)
  The Hangul syllables of the Galmuri7 pixel font by Lee Minseo (quiple), release v2.40.4,
  https://github.com/quiple/galmuri, as the 8 x 8 cell table site/fonts/galmuri7-hangul.bin
  written by tools/font/galmuri.py from the release's BDF. Licensed under the SIL Open Font
  License, Version 1.1: site/fonts/OFL.txt is the licence, site/fonts/NOTICE.txt names the
  source URL and the sha256s of the release zip and the table.

  Copyright (c) 2019-2025 Lee Minseo (quiple@quiple.dev)

Gowun Batang (site/fonts)
  The HD font: Gowun Batang Regular by Yanghee Ryu (The Gowun Batang Project Authors),
  https://github.com/yangheeryu/Gowun-Batang, the google/fonts repository's file subset to
  printable ASCII and the 11,172 Hangul syllables as the WOFF2 site/fonts/gowun-batang.woff2.
  Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1: site/fonts/OFL-gowun-batang.txt is the
  licence, site/fonts/NOTICE.txt names the source URL and the sha256s of the font and the subset.

  Copyright 2021 The Gowun Batang Project Authors (https://github.com/yangheeryu/Gowun-Batang)
