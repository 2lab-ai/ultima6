Controls
  • Walk: the arrow keys, or click the map
  • T talk, L look, G get, D drop, M move, U use
  • A attack, B combat, C cast, R rest
  • Space: pass a turn
  • Ctrl-S save, Ctrl-R restore
  • - and +: slower and faster
  • Space or Escape during a turn skips its remaining playback
  • F10: game settings
  • Escape skips the opening
