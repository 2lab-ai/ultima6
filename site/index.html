What this version does

The game starts as the original does: the opening (Escape skips it), then the menu, where C creates a character (or T transfers one from Ultima IV or V) and J journeys onward.

Works: walking (arrow keys), passing a turn (space), talking (T), combat (A attacks, B begins or breaks off combat), casting spells (C), resting (R), looking, getting, dropping, moving and using things (L, G, D, M, U), the inventory panel, the mouse (click the map to walk, the command icons, the party list, the inventory and the spellbook), saving and restoring the game (Ctrl-S, Ctrl-R), music and sound (Ctrl-Z turns the game's sound on and off; the Mute button silences the page). Sound starts once you click or press a key on the game screen.

Speed: on the game screen - slows the game and = (or +) speeds it up, through 1×, 1.5×, 2×, 3×, 4×, 6×, 8× and Max, but only while the game is not using that key: while you type a line or a name, in the stats and inventory panels (where - and + pick the member), in a party list long enough to scroll, and in the opening's menu, the key is the game's. The keypad - and + and the Speed buttons always change the speed. 1× is the original's pace; above 4× the music fades out. “behind” means this computer cannot keep up with the chosen speed.

HD (on by default; the HD button switches it): the map, the portraits and the panels are shown magnified two or four times with MMPX (Morgan McGuire and Mara Gagiu, MIT licence: copyright and permission notice), computed in your browser from your own files; text and the pointer stay the original pixels, scaled. An HD pack made from your own files with the project's local HD tool can be dropped here like the game files: its pictures replace MMPX for the images they were made from. It is kept in this browser (“Forget the HD pack” removes it) and never sent anywhere; this site hosts none.

Not yet: a few rare object actions (moving a carried thing to someone else, a staff's or wand's spell in combat). Those say “Not implemented in this version” or do nothing.

A character you create and a save (Ctrl-S) are kept in this browser and the next visit resumes it; “Forget the saved game” starts over. “Forget the kept files” removes the game files themselves.

Click the game screen or Tab to it before playing; Tab leaves it again. Your game files stay in your browser (kept in its storage for the next visit) and are never uploaded.
