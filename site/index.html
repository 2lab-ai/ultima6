What this version does

Works: walking (arrow keys), passing a turn (space), talking (T, a direction, then type and press Return), the mouse (click the map to walk, the command icons, the party list and the inventory). The clock runs and people follow their schedules.

Not yet: combat, magic, rest, the inventory commands (look, get, drop, move, use) and the inventory panel keys, saving and restoring a game, music and sound. Those commands say “Not implemented in this version” and do nothing. The game on the disks starts in combat mode and leaving it (B) is one of them, so party mode (0) and solo mode (1–9) are refused for now.

Keeping your files in this browser is not a saved game: every visit starts a new game from the disks.

Click the game screen or Tab to it before playing; Tab leaves it again. Your game files stay in your browser (kept in its storage for the next visit) and are never uploaded.
