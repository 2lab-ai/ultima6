Galmuri7 Hangul table (site/fonts/galmuri7-hangul.bin)

  The 11,172 Hangul syllables U+AC00..U+D7A3 of Galmuri7 by Lee Minseo (quiple), each as an
  8 x 8 cell of 8 row bytes (bit 7 = leftmost), in code point order, written by
  tools/font/galmuri.py from the font's BDF. The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font
