# 11 — HD font: text drawn at the HD layout's own size

Status: shipped (2026-10-23, in production at https://ultima6.2lab.ai from main ba3a639; deploys 3 and 4 of `hd-layout-font-register/loop.md`; Design 3 revised after deploy 3's captures). Not in this unit: F2 (Design 6). Rev 2 (2026-10-24, from deploy 15's captures): Design 7, the pointer drawn over the HD text; Design 2's exclusion of a cell under the pointer sprite is withdrawn; shipped in deploy 16 (main afaac84). Rev 3 (2026-10-25, from deploy 18's playtest in 한글 with the HD font, Wide and the owner's MacBook capture: the reply prompt's cursor about 110 canvas px right of its colon at 1920 × 1080, the more prompt's mark about two cells right of its text, a reply begun with two typed spaces drawn far right of its prompt): Design 2 lists the game's symbols drawn over the paper and the page draws one after a run as that run's caret; Design 3's spaces the player typed join the run. User item 2 of `hd-layout-font-register/ssot.md`, verbatim: "hd 해상도에 맞게 한글 폰트 개선해줘. 처음 설정에 니가 만든 기본 폰트랑 hd 화면에 맞는 적절한 폰트 사용해줘." It builds on 10 (the layout) and 08 (the Hangul cells).

## Measured facts (main 6e4e337)

- In the game program every character is one 8 × 8 cell drawn whole: set pixels in the window's ink, clear ones in its paper, colours 0..15 (`ui/text.rs:118-132` `draw_glyph`/`draw_rows`, `:140-157` `draw_char`). A code below 0x100 is a glyph of `u6.ch`; a Hangul syllable U+AC00..=U+D7A3 is its cell of the Hangul table (Galmuri7, OFL, `site/fonts/galmuri7-hangul.bin`, made by `tools/font/galmuri.py`, ink in rows 0..=6). The pixels are written with `Framebuffer::set`, so their source is classic (`u6-render/src/lib.rs:530`), and the HD compositor shows each one as an S × S block of its colour (`u6-render/src/hd.rs:686-780`): blocky text at HD.
- Scrolling a window moves pixels with their sources (`ui/text.rs:160-188`, `copy_px_within`).
- `u` (the opening, the story, creation) draws its text into the emulated chip planes (`intro/text.rs:195-250`, `intro/mem.rs:199`), which carry no source plane.
- `Src` is 12 bytes: `ver`, `id: u16`, `u`, `v`, `kind`, `flags` (`lib.rs:89-104`); `flags != 0` means the compositor shows the classic pixel.

## Design

1. **Engine: glyph sources.** `Kind` gains `Glyph` (4). `draw_rows` writes each pixel with `set_src`: kind `Glyph`, `id` the cell code (`u6.ch` code or the syllable's code point, both under 0x10000), `(u, v)` the pixel's place in the cell, `flags` a new `FLAG_INK` (4) on set pixels. The pixel colours are unchanged, so every parity receipt is bit-identical, and native and the tests still allocate no plane (07 Acceptance 1).
2. **Compositor: the HD font mode.** `set_hd_font(on)`. Off (the pixel font): a glyph pixel is shown as a classic pixel, as before, but over the paper (below). On: a cell is an HD glyph when all 64 of its pixels are `Glyph` sources of the same code with `(u, v)` running 0..7 over the cell, in one ink colour and one paper colour with both present (a solid block or an empty slot is not a letter), and its code is printable ASCII (0x21..=0x7e) or a Hangul syllable; its 64 blocks are filled with the cell's paper colour and the cell is listed. A space the text writer drew whole (code 0x20, all 64 pixels its paper, no ink) is listed too, as code 0x20 with its paper as ink: it is not drawn, it tells the page where a line of text goes on (Design 3). Any other glyph cell (runes; the game's own symbols, but those drawn over the paper, which are listed as symbols: rev 3, below) is not listed. The pointer sprite is not part of the frame the compositor reads (Design 7). Over the paper (07: the paper's pixels named in place), the HD paper shows through, only for glyph pixels drawn over it (`FLAG_PAPER`, set by `draw_rows` when the pixel it replaces is the paper's): a listed cell's block shows the HD paper wherever the paper's classic colour there equals the cell's paper colour, else the flat paper colour; a glyph pixel of a cell that is not listed and has no `FLAG_INK` shows the HD paper where its classic colour equals the paper's there. Text over a portrait or the spellbook is shown as before (follow-up P1, 2026-10-23). `hd_glyphs()` gives the whole list of the last composition (not a delta: a page that copies a dirty rect draws every listed glyph again over it): per cell its screen x and y, its code, its ink and its paper as RGB. A code the game uses as a symbol rather than a letter (the input cursor, the more prompt's mark, found in `ui/print.rs` and `ui/keyread.rs`) is never a character of the HD font.
   - **Symbols (rev 3).** Both symbols are drawn by `text::draw_symbol` from the key wait (`keyread.rs` `idle_wait`: glyphs 5..=8 the cursor, 1..=4 the more prompt's mark). Drawn over the paper, a glyph of the game's font names itself on its ink pixels: `Kind::Glyph` of its code, `(u, v)` their place in the cell, `FLAG_INK | FLAG_PAPER | FLAG_SYMBOL` (32). Those are the pixels the paper's rule flagged `FLAG_PAPER_OFF` before (or left classic where the paper drew nothing): shown classic either way, so the frame with the HD font off is byte for byte the same; its clear pixels name the paper as before, and so does an ink pixel of the paper's own colour there. On, a cell whose ink pixels are such sources of one code under 0x100 in one colour, and whose other pixels all name the paper at their place in one other colour (or in the ink's, unflagged: that ink pixel), is filled with its paper like a listed letter (a pixel of it that names the paper shows the paper's HD block only where the paper's colour there is the cell's paper colour, else the flat paper colour: so the ink pixel of the paper's own colour shows the cell's paper, not the paper's own speck; K12 review round 2) and listed as code `0xE000` + its glyph (private use; the page's list, `Game.hd_glyphs`, gives `0x10000 | glyph`), its rows (8 bytes, bit 7 leftmost) in `symbol_rows()` in the list's order (`Game.hd_symbol_rows`). A cell of it written again otherwise is not listed and shows as before. Not over the paper (over the spellbook) or with no paper, the symbol is classic and not listed, as before. Receipts: `gfx/tests/plane.rs` `the_key_wait_symbol_names_the_paper_under_it` (over the paper: its sources and its listing; over the spellbook and without the paper's colours: classic, unlisted) and `ui/tests/wide_tests.rs` `the_scroll_names_its_glyphs_and_the_hd_font_lists_its_letters` (a key wait with no paper: the cursor classic, the letters alone listed).
3. **Page: drawing the HD glyphs.** After the layout (10), the listed cells are drawn on the visible canvas in runs. A run is listed cells of one row of one region, each the next one's left neighbour, of one paper colour; one listed space joins the cells either side of it, two or more end the run (so the game's columns keep their places), and so does a cell not listed. A run is drawn as one line of text in the chosen HD font at about 0.85 of the cell's height, from 1 canvas pixel inside the left edge of its first cell that is not a space, each character in its own ink (a keyword keeps its colour), at the font's natural width, without kerning; a run wider than its cells less 1 pixel at each end (the browser's anti-aliasing reaches about a pixel past the ink box it reports) is narrowed to that (horizontal scale only). Revised 2026-10-23 from the first build's captures in game: each character centred in its cell, ASCII drawn 1.45 times as wide, read letter-spaced ("A v a t a r") and set a comma apart from the word it ends; the 1.45 had been chosen on a made-up sample. The page waits for the font (`document.fonts.load`) and draws the pixel font until it has it.
   - **Spaces the player typed (rev 3).** The line editor's echo (`keyread::echo`, 0x105d78) names each cell it draws `FLAG_TYPED` (64, on every pixel of the cell, `text::draw_typed`); the blank Backspace draws and the cursor's erase are the game's spaces. A whole space typed is listed as `0xE120` (the page's list: `0x20000 | 0x20`): it never ends a run, whatever the spaces beside it, and is drawn as a font space, so a reply begun with spaces stays one line with its prompt, also after a scroll (the sources move with the pixels). The game's own two or more spaces keep ending a run. A letter typed is listed as its letter (nothing on the page differs for it; the compositor's 16-bit code has no room for the syllables twice). The engine takes what it took: a leading space is the original's (0x107cba).
   - **The caret (rev 3).** A symbol listed right after a run of the same row and paper, or after the spaces (typed or not, any number) that follow its last character, is that run's caret: the page draws its pixels in its ink, its cell's left edge where the run's text and those spaces end (their advances in the HD font at the run's narrowing, `measureText`, never a count of cells: a syllable and a colon are narrower than their cells by different amounts), on the run's row, each pixel a block with its edges on whole canvas pixels; its own cell shows the paper (the compositor's fill: the paper's HD block where the paper there is the cell's paper colour, the flat paper colour elsewhere, so nothing of the symbol stays there; receipt `ui/tests/text_tests.rs` `a_symbols_old_cell_over_a_paper_of_two_colours_is_the_paper_alone`). A symbol after no run (the echo wrapped to a new row) is drawn at its own cell. A turn of the cursor changes its cell in the list, so its region is copied whole and its runs drawn again, as for any listed cell (a region holding text was copied whole each turn before rev 3 too).
4. **The HD font.** Gowun Batang (고운바탕, SIL OFL 1.1, Google Fonts), subset to printable ASCII and the 11,172 Hangul syllables, as WOFF2 under `site/fonts/` with its licence beside it, as Galmuri7 is. Chosen for the game's archaic register (08): a book face, readable at the message scroll's 24 to 30 px.
5. **The choice.** The first-run chooser (`#setup`) and Settings gain "Font: HD font / Pixel font" ("글꼴: HD 글꼴 / 기본 픽셀 글꼴"), HD by default, kept in `localStorage` (`u6.font`). The HD font applies only with the HD layout (10); with Original or HD off the pixel font is drawn.
6. **Not in this unit:** `u`'s text (the opening caption, the story, the gypsy, creation) stays in the pixel font until a source plane reaches the chip planes (follow-up F2).
7. **The pointer over the text (rev 2).** Deploy 15's frames (and deploy 14's) show the fault Design 2 caused. The engine draws the pointer sprite into the frame the compositor reads (`engine.rs` `tick`: `shown` = the published frame with `SpriteFrame::draw` over it, `ui/pointer.rs:260-273`, `Framebuffer::set`, a classic source). So a text cell under the sprite fails `glyph_cell` (`u6-render/src/hd.rs:613`) and is not listed. It shows the pixel font, and the run it stood in is cut in two (Design 3: a cell not listed ends a run). The first part is drawn at the font's natural width from its first cell, which leaves a gap before the cell under the pointer. The original's pointer is a hardware sprite above the screen (`ui/pointer.rs:7-9`). So:
   - **Engine.** `Engine::frame()` stays the published frame with the sprite: the classic RGBA frame, shown with HD off, is unchanged. The engine also gives the published frame without the sprite and the sprite it drew last (position, rows, and the shown frame's palette for its colours 16 + v).
   - **Compositor.** The HD frame is composed from the published frame without the sprite, so the glyph list and every HD block are the screen as the game drew it. A pointer move alone composes no block, adds no HD dirty rect and lists the same glyphs.
   - **`Game` (u6-web).** It gives the sprite's box in frame pixels, clipped to the frame (empty when there is none, the pointer is hidden, or HD is off), its pixels as RGBA8 (transparent where the shape's value is 0), and a version that moves when the box or the pixels change.
   - **Page.** Both HD paint paths draw the pointer last, above the HD font's characters. These are the HD layout's `paint` (its regions and the whole view) and Wide's `paintWide` (the map, the windows and the whole view). The sprite's box is mapped through every region whose source it meets, as that region maps the frame's pixels, pixelated and clipped to the region. So the pointer lands where the frame's own pixels of that place land, as now. When the pointer's version moves, the page first repaints the canvas under its old and new boxes, as for an HD dirty rect of those boxes: the regions they touch and the runs listed there. Then it draws the pointer.
   - **HD off.** Nothing changes: the classic frame holds the sprite.

## Acceptance

1. Synthetic tests: a window's text gives `Glyph` sources with `FLAG_INK` on ink pixels and unchanged colours; a scroll keeps them; a cell half overwritten by a tile or by a classic write is not listed; a Hangul and an ASCII cell are listed with their ink and paper; a whole space is listed as a space, a space with ink and a code under 0x20 are not.
2. HD font on: the message scroll and the side panel show the HD font in Korean and in English, at the layout's scale; off, they show the pixel font exactly as before (the HD paper behind its clear pixels where they are drawn over the paper, 07 P1). A line's characters stand at the font's natural spacing, a word's punctuation beside it, and two columns of one row keep their places.
3. The chooser and Settings keep the choice across a reload.
4. Gates pass; live check on the deployed site: a fresh visitor, the zip only, Korean, sees the HD font in game and can switch to the pixel font.
5. Rev 2:
   - **Synthetic.** A frame with a line of listed cells and the pointer sprite over one of them lists every cell of the line, as with the pointer away, and its HD frame holds no sprite pixel. This fails on main 5963bda, where the cell under the sprite is missing.
   - **Web tests,** in the HD layout and in Wide. With the pointer at rest over a line of text, the canvas shows the pointer's colours in its box. Outside the box, the canvas is the same as the same frame painted with the pointer away. After the pointer moves off, its old box is the same as a whole repaint.
   - **Live.** On the deployed site, a message window line with the pointer over it reads whole.
6. Rev 3:
   - **Synthetic.** Two made-up letters over the paper and the key wait's symbol in the next cell list three cells, the third the symbol with its rows (two before rev 3); without the paper's colours two; with the HD font off the HD frame is byte for byte that of the symbol's sources before rev 3. Through the key waits over the paper, the line editor's cursor after a line and the more prompt's mark are each listed as a symbol. The echo names its cells typed (spaces, a letter, a syllable), Backspace's blank (read as it is drawn, over a typed space, a typed letter and back across a row's end) and the cursor's erase not; the typed spaces are listed apart. The platform's list gives `0x10000 | glyph` and `0x20000 | 0x20`, and the rows.
   - **Web tests,** in the HD layout's regions, its letterbox and Wide, on the test's own conversation (a made-up word typed), every place read from the canvas's pixels (the symbol's ink's observed left edge, the word's first ink) against the test's own measure of the pen's end (`measureText` in the page): at the reply prompt the cursor is the prompt's caret, its observed ink edge at the prompt's pen end (its glyph's own blank columns on) within a canvas pixel, its own cell the paper; two typed spaces and the word make one run with the prompt, the word's first ink where the pen's end and two space advances put it (within the font's fringe); two spaces typed after the word move the caret's edge on by two measured advances and Backspace back by one, within a pixel; the echo wrapped puts the cursor alone at the next row's first cell; after Return and the scroll the typed line keeps its joined form. At a key wait inside the test's greeting the more prompt's mark is the caret of the text before it, its edge likewise. At the command prompt the cursor turns as the prompt's caret, each run drawn once. In the Original layout nothing is listed and the canvas is the HD frame exactly. Two broken pages fail them: the caret at its own cell, and the caret one space advance on (K12 review round 1).
